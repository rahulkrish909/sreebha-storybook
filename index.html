About the author

Sreebha Sasidharan

Sreebha Sasidharan creates warm, imaginative stories for children—stories that celebrate kindness, curiosity, friendship and the small discoveries that make growing up special.

The Unusual Wish is a gentle reminder that being different can be a beautiful part of being ourselves.

“Sometimes, trying something different helps us understand ourselves in a new way.”
